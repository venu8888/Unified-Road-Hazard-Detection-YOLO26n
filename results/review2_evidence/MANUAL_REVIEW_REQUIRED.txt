ISSUE 2: ARCHITECTURAL RESOLUTION OF D70 (GENERIC MANHOLE) VS MANHOLE CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Audit Status: SEMANTIC HIERARCHY CONFLICT IDENTIFIED
Details:
- Primary Dataset (N-RDD2024):
  D70 denotes a generic "manhole cover" object detector. Bounding boxes cover
  the manhole regardless of whether it is sound, cracked, sunken, or displaced.
  Instance count: 3,712 boxes across 2,767 images.
- Supplementary Dataset (Roboflow v4):
  Contains fine-grained condition annotations for manholes:
  - Good (685 boxes)
  - Broken (183 boxes)
  - Lose [Loose] (479 boxes)
  - Uncovered (289 boxes)
- Semantic Conflict:
  Mapping N-RDD D70 -> "manhole_good" would be false and unsupported (violating
  Safety Rule 7).
  Collapsing all supplementary condition classes into generic D70 would discard
  the exact critical condition labels needed to detect loose, broken, or uncovered
  hazards.
Options and Recommendation:
- See results/ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md for full analysis and recommendation:
  Hierarchical two-stage architecture or unified 10-class taxonomy with D70 as
  "manhole_unspecified / generic" alongside specific hazard conditions.
